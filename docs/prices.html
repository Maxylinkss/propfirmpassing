Not published means not published. A blank in that column is not a missing data point on our side — the provider does not list a price for that account size publicly. Where a provider quotes per client, the only way to get the figure is to ask, and the only thing to check when you get it is whether it still holds at the size you actually want.

Pound figures are converted to US dollars at the rate in use on the date the price was read. Treat any converted figure as approximate and check the provider's own page before relying on it.

Charge per funded account, not charge per attempt

The fee is only half the number. The other half is what a failed attempt costs, and most providers do not publish it. A provider charging $400 with full-price retries is a more expensive service than one charging $588 where retries are covered, and no comparison table shows that, because the retry term lives in checkout or in a support reply.

Two questions settle it:

  1. If the evaluation fails while you are running it, what do you owe for the next attempt?
  2. Does that answer change at $200,000, where two of the five providers raise their price?

Until the retry term is stated in writing, a fee comparison is comparing attempt one and calling it the price of the service.
